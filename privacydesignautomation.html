Privacy Design & Automation Advisory

We help organizations design automation-first privacy workflows that minimize manual effort, reduce compliance risk, and enable faster responses to regulatory requests. Through comprehensive assessments of your systems, data flows, and operational processes, we bridge the gap between legal compliance, technology systems, and business operations. Our guidance ensures privacy is embedded from the ground up. Reviews and recommendations align with global and local data protection regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA, providing a robust foundation for sustainable compliance.

Our Approach

  1. Workflow & Process Discovery: Identify current workflows and operational processes.
  2. Automation Opportunity Mapping: Determine where automation can reduce manual effort and improve efficiency.
  3. System & Tool Alignment: Ensure privacy technology supports compliance and operational goals.
  4. Governance & Risk Controls: Embed privacy and compliance measures into business operations
  5. Discovery & Scoping: Assess regulatory obligations, operational needs, and existing systems.
  6. Gap Analysis & Risk Mapping: Identify gaps between current practices and regulatory requirements.
  7. Technical & Design Review: Evaluate system design and technical processes for privacy readiness.
  8. Recommendations & Roadmap: Provide actionable steps to achieve privacy by design.
